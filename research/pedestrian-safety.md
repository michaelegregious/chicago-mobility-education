## Chicago yielding observations

- Active Transportation Alliance, 2014, pedestrian-yielding observations: https://activetransportation.org/media/compliance-with-must-stop-for-pedestrians-law-very-low-in-chicagoland/
- Earlier discussion identified 208 crossing trials at 52 locations in Chicago and nearby suburbs, with reported first-driver stopping rates of approximately 5% at unmarked crosswalks, 18% at painted crosswalks, and 61% at crossings with additional safety features. **Recheck denominators, methodology, dates, and exact wording before citation.**

## Comparative culture research

- Look for methodologically comparable yielding observations in Portland, Oregon, and Chicago.
- Avoid attributing city differences to culture without controlling for street design, speeds, traffic volume, enforcement, and measurement methodology.
- Explore descriptive norms (what people see others doing) versus injunctive norms (what people believe ought to be done).

## Potential program evaluation

- Repeat standardized yielding observations before and after implementation of recurring mobility education.
- Measure behavioral outcomes, not only video completion and quiz performance.
- Account for concurrent infrastructure and enforcement changes when interpreting results.
