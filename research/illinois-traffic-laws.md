## Pedestrian crossings

- Illinois Vehicle Code, 625 ILCS 5/11-1002(a): https://www.ilga.gov/Legislation/ILCS/Fulltext?DocName=062500050K11-1002
- At crosswalks without operating traffic-control signals, drivers must stop and yield when a pedestrian is on their half of the roadway, or approaching so closely from the opposite half as to be in danger.
- This does **not** mean every vehicle in both directions is legally required to stop whenever a pedestrian is anywhere in the roadway. The video may recommend a more cautious practice, but must distinguish advice from legal requirements.
- Research definition and exceptions for unmarked crosswalks, and verify Chicago-specific provisions.
- Research passing a vehicle stopped at a crosswalk and signalized-turn obligations.
